## The Hiring Bottleneck

Building an in-house engineering team takes months. Between sourcing, interviewing, negotiating, and onboarding, most startups burn 3-6 months before their first hire writes production code. For a company racing to validate a market hypothesis, that timeline is a luxury they can't afford.

## Cost Isn't the Only Factor

While cost savings are real — especially when working with global talent — the bigger advantage is speed. An experienced outsourcing partner can assemble a team and start delivering within days, not months.

## When to Outsource vs. Hire

Outsourcing works best when you need to move fast on well-defined projects: an MVP, a mobile app, a platform migration. Once you've found product-market fit and need a team deeply embedded in your domain, that's when in-house hiring starts to make more sense.

## The Hybrid Approach

Many of our most successful clients use a hybrid model: a small in-house team focused on core product decisions, augmented by outsourced engineers who handle execution. This gives them the strategic depth of internal talent with the speed and flexibility of external support.
